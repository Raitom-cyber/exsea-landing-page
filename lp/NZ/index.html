How we work

Full photos and the auction / inspection sheet, then documents for smooth clearance.

What you get

  • Japanese export certificate (de-registration) with the 3-digit emissions code — for NZTA entry certification
  • Inspection sheet where available (original on request); grade 4 or better preferred for NZ
  • Odometer reading at sale and at shipment, written on the bill of lading
  • ESC and frontal-impact compliance confirmed by model code before payment
  • MPI biosecurity cleaning through an approved system before loading
  • CO2 figure for the Clean Car Standard on request

Good to know

Not included: NZ GST (15%), Clean Car Standard charge, entry certification and local costs.
